1. MARKS COVERED

This policy covers the following, referred to together as the "Brand":

- the name "VOLDBASE" and the form "Voldbase";
- the VOLDBASE logo, logotype and wordmark;

  screenshots used as branding, and the package name and command name
  `voldbase` as used to identify the official Software;
- any confusingly similar name, mark or design used for software that is or
  claims to be VOLDBASE.

The Brand belongs to the project owner. This policy does not transfer, license
or waive any right in it.

2. RELATION TO THE LICENSE

`LICENSE` grants rights in software, not in the Brand (section 10). A permission
to use, copy or (with written permission) distribute the Software does not
include a permission to use the Brand beyond what this policy allows.

3. PERMITTED USE WITHOUT ASKING

You may use the "VOLDBASE" name without prior permission only for accurate,
factual, non-misleading references:

3.1 Referring to the Software by its name in articles, reviews, tutorials,
    classroom material, academic papers, talks and comparison tables.

3.2 Stating truthfully that your work uses, targets, is compatible with, or
    integrates with VOLDBASE — for example "uses VOLDBASE", "built on
    VOLDBASE", "a VOLDBASE experiment definition".

3.3 Redistributing an unmodified copy of the Software under `LICENSE`
    section 4.1(c), keeping its name and notices intact, as long as you do not
    suggest that you are the project or its maintainer.

3.4 Linking to the official repository, or referring to it by URL.

3.5 Nominative and other uses that law permits without authorization, such as
    identifying the origin of the Software.

4. NOT PERMITTED WITHOUT PRIOR WRITTEN PERMISSION

4.1 Using the Brand, or anything confusingly similar, as the name or mark of

    media account or app-store listing.

4.2 Using the Brand in a way that states or implies sponsorship, endorsement,
    partnership, certification or official affiliation with the VOLDBASE
    project or its owner, including wording such as "official", "certified",

4.3 Distributing a modified version under the VOLDBASE name, or under a name
    that suggests it is the official Software. A modified version may only be

    section 4.2, and must be presented under a different name, with a clear
    statement that it is modified and not the official Software.

4.4 Using the logo, logotype, icon or visual identity in your own product,
    website, repository, documentation, merchandise, advertising or
    presentation, beyond a factual reference under section 3.

4.5 Registering or using the Brand, or a confusingly similar mark, as a
    trademark, business name, domain name, package name, or username.

4.6 Altering, recoloring, distorting or animating the logo, or combining it with

4.7 Using the Brand in a way that disparages the project or is unlawful,
    deceptive or misleading.

5. NAMING FORKS AND DERIVATIVES

If you are permitted to distribute a Modification, you must:

5.1 give it a different name that does not include "VOLDBASE" or a confusingly
    similar term;
5.2 state clearly, in the distribution and in its documentation, that it is a
    modified version and that it is not affiliated with or endorsed by the
    VOLDBASE project;
5.3 keep the copyright notices, `LICENSE`, `NOTICE` and this policy intact;
5.4 not use the logo or visual identity as the derivative's branding.

A factual statement of the form "derived from VOLDBASE" is permitted and
encouraged.

Nothing in this policy states or implies that the Brand is registered as a

other rights, and this policy is not a statement about the scope of those
rights.

7. REQUESTING PERMISSION

Requests for use outside section 3 go to the project owner through the
repository owner account on GitHub (github.com/Zakariaika). State the mark you
want to use, the context, the audience, and the duration. Permission must be in
writing and is limited to the use described; it does not extend to other uses,
other marks, or other periods.
