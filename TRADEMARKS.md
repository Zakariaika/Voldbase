VOLDBASE — TRADEMARK AND BRAND USE POLICY

Status: draft, 2026-11-19. This policy is part of the licensing documentation but
is not itself a trademark licence. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It does
not state that any mark is registered, and it does not claim rights beyond those
that actually exist.

1. WHAT THIS POLICY COVERS

This policy addresses the project's names and identifiers, together referred to
as the "Brand":

- the project name "VOLDBASE" and the form "Voldbase", as an identifier of this
  project and its official distributions;
- any logo, logotype or wordmark the project adopts. The project does not
  currently distribute a logo file; if one is adopted, this policy applies to it
  from that point;
- a name, mark or design that is confusingly similar to the above, where it is
  used to suggest that a product is this project or is connected with it.

The following are NOT trademarks or brand elements, and this policy does not
claim them as marks:

- the project's colour palette, typography, iconography and product screenshots;
  those may be protected by copyright or other rights, and using them deceptively
  may be misleading, but they are not asserted here as trademarks;
- the package name and the command name `voldbase`, which are identifiers of the
  official distribution. This policy addresses only their use in a way that
  falsely suggests an official or affiliated product, not their use to refer to,
  invoke or remain compatible with the Software.

2. COPYRIGHT IN ARTWORK IS SEPARATE FROM TRADEMARK RIGHTS

Copyright in logo artwork, if any exists, belongs to the project owner and is
separate from any trademark or brand right in the name or mark. A copyright
licence in artwork is not a trademark licence, and a trademark permission is not
a copyright licence.

No trademark registration is claimed in any jurisdiction. The project owner
asserts only whatever protection unregistered marks, passing off, unfair
competition or consumer-protection law actually gives in the relevant place.
This policy is not evidence that a mark is registered, distinctive, enforceable
or owned exclusively, and it does not describe the scope of any such right.

3. RELATION TO THE LICENCE

`LICENSE` grants rights in software, not in the Brand (`LICENSE` section 10). A
permission to use, copy or distribute the Software does not include a permission
to use the Brand beyond what this policy allows. Conversely, this policy grants
no right in the Software.

4. PERMITTED USE WITHOUT ASKING

You may use the "VOLDBASE" name without prior permission for accurate, factual,
non-misleading references, including:

4.1 Referring to the Software by its name in articles, reviews, criticism,
    tutorials, classroom material, academic papers, talks, bug reports and
    comparison tables.

4.2 Stating truthfully that your work uses, targets, is compatible with, or
    integrates with VOLDBASE — for example "uses VOLDBASE", "built on
    VOLDBASE", "a VOLDBASE experiment definition".

4.3 Redistributing an unmodified copy of the Software under `LICENSE` section
    4.1(c), keeping its name and notices intact, as long as you do not suggest
    that you are the project or its maintainer.

4.4 Linking to the official repository, or referring to it by URL.

4.5 Criticism, review, news reporting, commentary, comparison and satire or
    parody, to the extent the law permits. You do not need permission to
    criticise VOLDBASE, to publish a negative review, to report on the project,
    or to compare it unfavourably with something else. Truthful statements about
    the project are permitted even if the project owner disagrees with them.

4.6 Nominative and other uses that law permits without authorisation, such as
    identifying the origin of the Software or using the name where no realistic
    alternative exists.

5. NOT PERMITTED WITHOUT PRIOR WRITTEN PERMISSION

5.1 Using the Brand, or anything confusingly similar, as the name or mark of
    your product, service, company, package, organisation, domain name, social
    media account or app-store listing, in a way that would mislead people about
    the origin of what they are getting.

5.2 Using the Brand in a way that falsely states or implies sponsorship,
    endorsement, partnership, certification or official affiliation with the
    VOLDBASE project or its owner, including wording such as "official",
    "certified" or "approved by VOLDBASE", or a logo lockup with another
    product's mark, where that statement is untrue or misleading.

5.3 Distributing a modified version under the VOLDBASE name, or under a name
    that suggests it is the official Software. A modified version may be
    distributed non-commercially only if it is presented under a different name,
    uses none of the official identity, and says clearly that it is modified,
    unofficial and not affiliated with the project (`LICENSE` section 4.2).
    Commercial distribution requires the prior written permission required by
    `LICENSE` section 5.

5.4 Using a logo, logotype or wordmark, if the project adopts one, as your own
    product's branding, or in a way that suggests your product is the official
    Software or is endorsed by it.

5.5 Registering or using the Brand, or a confusingly similar mark, as a
    trademark, business name, domain name, package name or account name, in
    order to trade on the project's identity or to prevent the project from
    using its own name.

5.6 Altering, recolouring, distorting or animating a logo in a way that creates a
    misleading impression of official affiliation or endorsement.

6. NAMING FORKS AND DERIVATIVES

If you distribute a Modification — which `LICENSE` section 4.2 permits for
non-commercial use — you must:

6.1 give it a different name that does not include "VOLDBASE" or a confusingly
    similar term;
6.2 state clearly, in the distribution and in its documentation, that it is a
    modified version and that it is not affiliated with or endorsed by the
    VOLDBASE project;
6.3 keep the copyright notices, `LICENSE`, `NOTICE` and this policy intact;
6.4 not use the project's logo or visual identity as the derivative's branding.

A factual statement of the form "derived from VOLDBASE" is permitted and
encouraged. Truthful statements that a fork exists, what it changes, and why,
are permitted.

7. REQUESTING PERMISSION

Requests for use outside section 4 go to the project owner through the
repository owner account on GitHub (github.com/Zakariaika). State the mark you
want to use, the context, the audience, and the duration. Permission must be in
writing and is limited to the use described; it does not extend to other uses,
other marks, or other periods.

8. ENFORCEMENT AND CHANGES

The project owner may revise this policy. A revision applies to uses that begin
after it is published; it does not retroactively withdraw a use that was
permitted when it began. Failure to enforce a provision is not a waiver. Nothing
in this policy restricts a use that the law permits without authorisation, and
nothing in it should be read as an assertion that a mark is registered or
exclusive beyond what the law provides.
